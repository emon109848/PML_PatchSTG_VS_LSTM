Model, selection and provenance

This replay is illustrative, not a new benchmark. It uses chronologically selected origins with no quality filtering. Do not tune using test examples. PatchSTG is a shared-protocol adaptation; the LSTM is the project’s contextual temporal model.



716 sensors · 15-minute cadence · 12 → 12 frames. Contextual LSTM and adapted PatchSTG. Same data protocol; different architectures and information. Playback reads cached forecasts; it does not run models in your browser.
